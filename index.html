Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Developer: AkshayGames

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Summary

Woodora is a puzzle game that works without an account. Your name, avatar and game progress stay on your own device. We (AkshayGames) do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some device information, such as your device’s advertising ID. Section 4 explains this and how to control it.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile game Woodora (Android package com.akshaygames.woodora), published by AkshayGames (“we”, “us”). It explains what information the game uses and how it is handled.

2. Information you enter in the game

When you first open the game you may enter:

  • a player name (any nickname you choose, up to 12 characters);
  • a boy / girl choice, used only to suggest matching avatar pictures;
  • your chosen avatar picture and frame.

This information is saved only on your device so the game can show it on your profile. It is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. You can change your name and avatar at any time in the Profile screen.

3. Game data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file in its private storage on your device containing:

  • levels you have cleared and your best IQ score;
  • coins, gems and hint coupons you have earned, and the avatars and frames you have unlocked;
  • the date of your last daily reward and your current daily streak;
  • your sound, music and vibration settings.

We do not upload this data to any server. If your phone’s own Google backup is turned on, Android may include this file in your personal device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. The data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Woodora is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The game shows a full-screen ad between some levels. Watching a reward video, for a free hint or double coins, is always optional.

When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location (country or city);
  • device and app information, such as phone model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalised ads. The data is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads in Woodora are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

5. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools.
  • The leaderboard is calculated on your device against built-in computer players; your data is not uploaded.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state – needed to load ads.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob as described in section 4.
  • Vibration – for short haptic feedback when blocks are placed. You can turn it off in Settings.

7. Links to other apps and services

The Settings screen has a Share button that opens Woodora’s page on Google Play, and a Feedback button that opens your email app. These are handled by those apps and services under their own privacy policies.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we will delete your message on request.

8. Children

Woodora is a family-friendly puzzle game, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s own policies. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

10. Your choices

  • Change your name or avatar at any time in the Profile screen.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available), or control personalised ads in your phone settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app’s data in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the game.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the game handles information, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy before that update is released and change the effective date above.
